# Dataset setup

Download `PS_20174392719_1491204439457_log.csv` from:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ealaxi/paysim1

Place it in this directory. Do not commit the large raw CSV to Git.

PaySim is synthetic. Its results are not evidence about real customers or real-world fraud.
The Kaggle dataset listing identifies the license as CC BY-SA 4.0; credit the authors and
review license requirements before redistributing data or adaptations.

Citation: Lopez-Rojas, E. A., Elmir, A., & Axelsson, S. (2016).
"PaySim: A financial mobile money simulator for fraud detection." EMSS.
